Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know about OCTOQUIZPUS — from how to play and submit questions to offline support and Android apps.

What is OCTOQUIZPUS?
OCTOQUIZPUS is a collection of free, interactive trivia quiz games covering topics like Bitcoin, board games, and chemical elements. All quizzes run in the browser and are available as Progressive Web Apps. Some are also on Google Play.
Are the quizzes free to play?
Yes — all quizzes on OCTOQUIZPUS are completely free. Play directly in your browser or install any quiz as a PWA on your device at no cost.
Can I play the quizzes offline?
Yes. Once you have opened a quiz in your browser you can install it as a PWA and play offline. The Android apps from Google Play also work without an internet connection.
What topics are covered?
Current topics include Bitcoin & cryptocurrency, board games, and chemical elements. More topics are planned — check back regularly for new additions.
Is OCTOQUIZPUS available on Android?
Yes. The Bitcoin Trivia Quiz and Board Game Trivia Quiz are on Google Play. All quizzes can additionally be installed as a PWA from Chrome on any Android device.
How can I submit my own trivia question?
Use the Submit a Question form to contribute your own trivia question. Every submission is reviewed before it goes live. Accepted questions appear on the Community Questions page and may make it into future quiz editions.
Where can I browse community-submitted questions?
Head over to the Community Questions page to browse all approved questions submitted by other players.
How do I install a quiz as a PWA?
Open the quiz in Chrome (or any Chromium-based browser), tap the install icon in the address bar or use the browser menu and select "Install app" / "Add to Home screen". The quiz then runs like a native app — even offline.
Who made OCTOQUIZPUS?
