About the Bitcoin Trivia Quiz

What topics does the Bitcoin Trivia Quiz cover?
Questions span the full spectrum of Bitcoin knowledge: the history of Bitcoin and Satoshi Nakamoto, blockchain fundamentals, mining and halving events, wallets and keys, the Lightning Network, notable price milestones, and broader cryptocurrency concepts.
How many questions are included?
The quiz features a large pool of questions across multiple difficulty levels — beginner, intermediate, and advanced — so every session is a fresh challenge whether you are new to Bitcoin or a long-time HODLer.
Is the Bitcoin Trivia Quiz free?
Yes, completely free. Play in the browser at bitcoin.triviaquiz.games or download the Android app from Google Play — no purchase or subscription required.
Can I play offline?
Yes. Install the PWA from your browser (tap the address bar install icon) and all questions are cached locally for offline play. The Android app also works without an internet connection.
Can I submit my own Bitcoin trivia question?
Absolutely! Use the Submit a Question form to contribute. All submissions are reviewed and approved questions appear on the Community Questions page.
