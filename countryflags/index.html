About the Country Flags Trivia Quiz

What does the Country Flags Trivia Quiz cover?
The quiz tests your knowledge of all 195 world flags. Questions include "identify the flag from its image", flag colours and symbols, capitals and continents, and tricky similar-looking flags from different countries.
Is it suitable for geography students?
Absolutely. The Country Flags Quiz makes geography revision fun and interactive. It covers flags of UN member states, territories, and dependencies across all continents — great for students, travellers, and geography enthusiasts.
Is the Country Flags Trivia Quiz free?
Yes, completely free. Play directly at countryflags.triviaquiz.games with no account or payment required.
Why is it labelled Beta?
The Country Flags Quiz is actively being developed with more question types and features on the way. Have a suggestion? Use the Submit a Question form to contribute.
Can I play offline?
Yes. Install the PWA via the browser address bar and all content is cached locally for offline play — perfect for quizzing on the go.
